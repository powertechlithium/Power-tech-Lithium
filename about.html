Our Profile

MS Powertech – Lithium Battery Solutions

MS Powertech is a lithium battery solutions business focused on manufacturing and supplying reliable lithium battery packs for electric mobility and energy storage applications.

We specialize in LiFePO4 battery technology, using A+ grade lithium cells and offering smart and standard Battery Management System (BMS) configurations.

Our core production line manufactures 3 Standard Flagship Models (48V 20Ah, 51V 30Ah, and 60V 30Ah) for electric two-wheelers with fast turnaround, while all other applications—including electric rickshaw batteries, home inverter energy storage, and industrial packs—are expertly Made by Custom according to client requirements.

With a focus on customer requirements, manufacturing flexibility, and sustainable energy solutions, MS Powertech aims to support the growing demand for advanced battery technology.

Quality In Every Step

From Quality Cells to Reliable Battery Packs

Our structured manufacturing process ensures high safety, precise cell balance, and mechanical durability.

01

1. Battery Cell Selection

Grade-A+ LiFePO4 cells are sorted and matched by internal resistance (IR), open-circuit voltage, and actual capacity before grouping.

02

2. Cell Configuration & Assembly

Cells are mounted into flame-retardant cell holders, secured with insulating barriers, and interconnected with high-conductivity busbars.

03

3. BMS Integration

Installation of Smart BMS (with active balancing & Bluetooth communication) or heavy-duty Standard BMS with multi-temperature sensors.

04

4. Battery Pack Enclosure

Housing the battery assembly into water-resistant, powder-coated sheet metal casing with vibration dampers, carry handles, and heavy connectors.

05

5. Quality Inspection & Testing

Full charge/discharge cycle testing, thermal validation, voltage symmetry verification, and BMS safety trigger confirmation.

06

6. Final Product Delivery

Protective shrink-wrapping, secure shock-resistant cardboard packaging, user documentation, and dispatch to wholesalers and clients.
